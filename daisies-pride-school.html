About Daisies Pride School

Inspiring Greatness, Building Character, Leading the Future.

Founded with a vision to revolutionize basic and secondary education in Nigeria, Daisies Pride School provides an enriching blend of the Nigerian and British Curricula, designed to spark intellectual curiosity and foster integrity.

Our Mission

To empower every learner through holistic education, critical thinking, and character development, preparing them to excel globally.

Our Vision

To be Nigeria's premier educational center, celebrated for academic distinction, moral excellence, and innovative leadership.
